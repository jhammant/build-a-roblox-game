The picker: how a child steers

The heart of the kit. Claude draws each part of the game three ways, in the game's real style, so what your child picks is what gets built. They tap their favourites on a tablet, and Claude reads the picks back and writes them up as exact specs for the build.

  • Three options, never more. Short names and a one-line description a child can read.
  • A note box on every board. The notes carried some of the best ideas.
  • Change your mind any time. The last tap counts, and every tap plays a happy note.
  • Saves as they tap, to a private Claude page or to your own computer over your home Wi-Fi. No accounts needed for the Wi-Fi version.
  • Ambiguous notes get asked about, not guessed. "All of them" could mean two things.
  • Rounds: the look first, then bad guys and places, then features, each one prompted by playtesting.

Get started

Setup takes about an hour, and you do it on your own. Then your child sits down to a game that already plays, and starts making it theirs.

You'll need

  • A Mac or Windows computer that runs Roblox Studio (the kit is tested on a Mac)
  • A Roblox account for you (it owns the game) and one for your child
  • A Claude plan that includes Claude Code
  • A tablet for picking, if you have one
# after installing Roblox Studio, Rokit and Claude Code
git clone https://github.com/jhammant/build-a-roblox-game.git
cd build-a-roblox-game
python3 new-game.py ~/Games/our-game

cd ~/Games/our-game
rokit install
claude          # then type /dream

The full walkthrough is in chapter 1 of the guide.

Questions parents ask

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Claude writes the code and explains what it did. You'll type a few commands in a terminal during setup, and it helps to be comfortable doing that. The guide gives you every command.

What does it cost?

The kit is free. Roblox Studio is free. Claude Code needs a Claude plan that includes it (Pro or above). The games it makes have no purchases in them.

Can my child's friends play it?

Your own child can, easily. As of October 2026, a new Roblox game reaches only age-checked players aged 16+ and your Trusted Friends until it passes Roblox's Kids & Select evaluation, so friends take some setting up. The guide explains each option, and the honest trade-offs.

How long does it take?

Gun Flower took about a day. Most families will go slower, in short sessions: 20 minutes of dreaming, 10 to 15 of picking, 15 to 20 of playing, with Claude building in between.

Is it safe?

The kit is built around it: your account owns the game, cartoon not gore, no purchases, chat off, no free text without Roblox's filtering, and family recordings stay at home. A safety check runs before every publish.

Is this made by Roblox or Anthropic?

No. It's an independent open-source project that works with Roblox Studio and Claude Code.
